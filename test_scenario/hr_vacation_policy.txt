# GlobalCorp Vacation Policy (2026)

## Paid Time Off (PTO)
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid vacation per year.
Vacation time accrues at a rate of 1.66 days per month.

## Carryover Policy
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused vacation days into the next calendar year.
Any carried-over days must be used by March 31st of the following year, or they will be forfeited.

## Requesting Time Off
All time off requests must be submitted through the HR Portal at least two weeks in advance.
Requests for more than 5 consecutive days require approval from a direct manager and the HR Director.

## Sick Leave
In addition to vacation days, employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave annually.
A doctor's note is required if an employee is out sick for three or more consecutive days.
