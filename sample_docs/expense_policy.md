# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Overview
Employees may submit expense reports for legitimate business-related expenses.

## Eligible Expenses
- Business travel (flights, hotels, ground transport)
- Client meals and entertainment (max $75/person)
- Conference registration fees (pre-approved)
- Professional development and training materials
- Required software subscriptions (pre-approved by IT)

## Submission Process
1. Collect itemized receipts for all expenses over $25
2. Submit via Expensify within **30 days** of incurring the expense
3. Include project code and business justification
4. Manager approval required for all submissions
5. VP approval required for expenses over **$500**

## Reimbursement Timeline
- Approved expenses are reimbursed within **2 pay cycles** (approximately 4 weeks)
- Direct deposit to payroll account

## Non-Reimbursable Items
- Personal meals during regular work hours
- Commuting costs (home to office)
- Personal electronics or accessories
- Alcohol (unless part of approved client entertainment)
- Traffic fines or parking violations
