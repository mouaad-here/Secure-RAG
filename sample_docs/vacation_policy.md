# Vacation Policy

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

All full-time employees are entitled to **20 days** of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly.

### Accrual Schedule
- **Year 1-3:** 20 days/year (1.67 days/month)
- **Year 4-7:** 25 days/year (2.08 days/month)
- **Year 8+:** 30 days/year (2.50 days/month)

### Requesting Time Off
1. Submit requests via the HR portal at least **2 weeks** in advance.
2. Requests over 5 consecutive days require **manager and HR approval**.
3. Holiday blackout periods may apply during peak business seasons.

### Carryover
- Up to **5 unused days** may carry over to the next calendar year.
- Carryover days must be used by **March 31** of the following year.
- Days beyond the carryover limit are forfeited.

### Sick Leave
- Employees receive **10 days** of paid sick leave per year.
- Sick leave does not accrue or carry over.
- A doctor's note is required for absences of **3 or more consecutive days**.
