# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their **90-day probation period** are eligible for remote work.

## Schedule Options
- **Hybrid:** Up to 3 days remote per week (default)
- **Fully Remote:** Requires VP-level approval and documented justification
- **On-Site Required:** Certain roles (e.g., facilities, hardware engineering) require on-site presence

## Equipment
The company provides:
- Laptop (refreshed every 3 years)
- One external monitor
- $500 home office setup stipend (one-time)

## Expectations
1. Maintain regular working hours (**9 AM – 5 PM** local time) or approved flex schedule
2. Be available on Slack/Teams during core hours (**10 AM – 3 PM**)
3. Attend all mandatory in-person meetings (typically 1/month)
4. Ensure a stable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps)

## Security Requirements
- Use company VPN for all work-related access
- Do not use public Wi-Fi without VPN
- Lock your screen when stepping away
- Report any security incidents to IT immediately
